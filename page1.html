CV & Professional Notice.

I’m a game designer with four years of experience developing engaging player-focused experiences. My expertise lies in systems design, gameplay mechanics, and prototyping, but I’ve worked across most aspects of game development.

Game development is an ever evolving field, and I’m always learning, experimenting, and honing my skills to push my work further. If that's refining a core gameplay loop or building something new, my passion remains the same: creating immersive, polished, and meaningful experiences for players.
