About

Brief

Jungle Drive is an endless runner style game where the player must navigate harsh terrain, build and maintain score, all without crashing.

Project Info

This game was developed as a short solo project during my second year at College. The game features infinite terrain generation, unlockable customisation, high score systems, unique environment interactions and more. All designed, modelled and scripted by me.
